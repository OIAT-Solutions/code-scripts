## 1. Objective and Recommended End State

Recover the historical Profit and Loss and Balance Sheet without deleting valid
QBO sales history, establish a verified opening inventory position, and prevent
negative QBO inventory and FIFO costing from distorting future COGS.

The recommended end state is:

- EPOS is the source of truth for physical quantities, pack relationships, and
  operational stock.
- A controlled Product Conversion List maps every EPOS sellable product to a
  stable canonical SKU and unit.
- Future EPOS sales are posted to canonical **Non-inventory** QBO sales items.
  They record revenue and VAT but do not change QBO quantity on hand or create
  automatic FIFO COGS.
- QBO holds the financial Inventory Asset balance and COGS. At each month end,
  verified inventory purchases and the EPOS closing valuation support a
  controlled COGS journal.
- Existing QBO inventory products and their historical transactions are
  preserved but excluded from future automation.

This avoids attempting to repair thousands of damaged historical QBO item
quantities in place.

## 3. Important Unit-of-Measure Rule

Product names alone are not sufficient to determine the multiplier. The
conversion must represent every physical level:

1. Outer/carton.
2. Retail pack.
3. Individual item.

For `BACKWOODS DARK STOUT CIGARS (5X1)*40`, the confirmed structure is:

- One retail pack contains **5 cigars**.
- One outer/carton contains **40 retail packs**.
- One outer/carton therefore contains **200 individual cigars**.

If EPOS reports 10 complete cartons and a current volume of 10 out of 40, and
that volume represents loose retail packs, the quantities are:

```text
Retail packs = (10 cartons x 40 packs) + 10 loose packs = 410 packs
Individual cigars = 410 packs x 5 cigars = 2,050 cigars
```

Any separately counted loose individual cigars are then added to 2,050.

Cost must follow the same hierarchy. If a verified carton cost were
NGN 85,000:

```text
Cost per retail pack = NGN 85,000 / 40 = NGN 2,125
Cost per cigar = NGN 85,000 / 200 = NGN 425
```

The current EPOS carton cost is NGN 85,000. The `5X1` product costs NGN 10,625,
which is five retail-pack units at NGN 2,125 each. However, the product labelled
`1X1` also costs NGN 2,125 while its Description says `5X1`. If `1X1` truly
means one cigar, its expected cost would be NGN 425. The family therefore stays
blocked until staff confirms what each till button physically sells and checks
the child Master Product Amounts.

The same multi-level rule will be applied to every product family. A single
`*N` name-based multiplier will not be treated as sufficient evidence.

### How inner package counts will be interpreted

An inner count is not automatically an inventory multiplier. It depends on the
smallest unit that the business actually sells and counts.

Examples:

- If a diaper product is sold only as a sealed `10pcs` retail bag and a carton
  contains 10 bags, the stock conversion is carton-to-bag `10`. The ten diapers
  inside the bag are product content, not ten separately tracked stock units.
- If Backwoods is sold both as one cigar and as a five-cigar pack, the smallest
  sellable unit is one cigar. A carton containing 40 packs of five therefore
  contains 200 canonical units.
- If Mimosa `4*125g` is sold only as the four-pot retail pack, the four pots are
  content and the canonical stock unit can remain the retail pack. If individual
  pots are also sold, the canonical unit must instead be one pot.

The Product Conversion List will therefore store explicit conversion fields
rather than infer them from every number in the product name:

- `canonical_unit`
- `full_stock_unit_to_canonical`
- `current_volume_unit_to_canonical`
- `sale_unit_to_canonical`
- `purchase_unit_to_canonical`
- `content_count_not_stock`

For Backwoods, staff must confirm the **Master Product Amount** configured on
both the `1X1` and `5X1` child products. That will determine whether EPOS is
currently deducting cigars, five-cigar packs, or another unit when each child is
sold.

### EPOS standard for future product creation

Before a new product is made available on the till:

1. Decide the smallest unit that may be sold separately. This is the canonical
   unit.
2. Record the packaging hierarchy: purchase outer, retail pack, and canonical
   unit.
3. Track stock on one approved master product only.
4. Set the master `Volume of Sale` to the number of canonical units in one full
   tracked stock unit.
5. On every child product, set the `Master Product Amount` to the number of
   canonical units consumed by one sale.
6. Enter the verified purchase cost for the full tracked unit and derive the
   expected child costs from the conversion. Record any deliberate bundle
   discount separately from inventory cost.
7. Use an unambiguous name that states the unit, for example `EACH`,
   `PACK-OF-5`, or `CARTON-40-PACKS`.
8. Assign a stable SKU in the EPOS product SKU field. The supplied Product List
   exports contain no populated Order Codes or Article Codes, so confirm which
   export or API field carries the EPOS SKU before the pipeline relies on it.
9. Use the physical barcode only for the unit actually carrying that barcode.
   Do not rely on the same barcode to distinguish an each, pack, and carton.
10. Perform a test sale of every child and confirm the exact reduction on the
    master before the product is released.
11. Add the EPOS Product ID and SKU to the Product Conversion List.
12. Keep the product blocked from QBO automation until the mapping validation
    passes.

For the Backwoods family, the recommended EPOS configuration—if both individual
cigars and five-cigar packs are sold—is:

```text
Canonical unit: 1 cigar
Full carton conversion: 40 packs x 5 cigars = 200 cigars
Loose retail pack conversion: 5 cigars
1X1 sale consumption: 1 cigar
5X1 sale consumption: 5 cigars
```

The master may either be configured directly as 200 canonical units, or as
40 retail packs with carefully configured fractional child consumption. The
200-unit approach is easier to audit when individual cigars are sold. The
existing child Master Product Amounts must be inspected before choosing or
changing the configuration.
